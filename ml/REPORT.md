# Forecast models: test results

Generated by `ml/train_forecasts.py` from `data/historical-prices.csv`. Do not edit by hand.

- **Two models, two ranges.** ARIMA forecasts the short term, the next 3 months: the app's sell-or-wait advice. LSTM forecasts the long term, the next 12 months: the app's price outlook.
- **Data:** monthly retail prices per kilo. The last 12 months of each variety were held back for testing.
- **Test:** each model was fitted on the earlier months, then asked at every step for its own range ahead (3 months for ARIMA, 12 for LSTM), using only what was known by then. The errors are over all of those forecasts together.
- **MAE:** the average miss, in pesos per kilo. **RMSE:** the same, with big misses counted more heavily. **MAPE:** the average miss as a percentage of the real price.
- **Same price** is the simplest forecast there is: the price stays where it is. A model is only useful where it beats it over the same range (✓).
